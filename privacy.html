Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who uses this app

Charles Inbox is a personal tool used only by its owner, Charles Edeline. It accesses only his own Gmail account, with his consent. No other person's account is ever connected.

What data it accesses

With the Gmail permissions the owner grants, the app reads incoming email, applies labels, archives mail and saves or sends reply drafts. It never permanently deletes email.

How the data is used

Email content is used only to sort the owner's mail, write short summaries and draft replies for the owner to approve. To do this, parts of an email may be sent to the Anthropic API (Claude) for processing. Summaries and drafts are shown to the owner in a private Telegram chat.

Storage

The app runs on the owner's own home server. It stores message identifiers, short summaries, drafts and the owner's decisions. Full email bodies are not stored. Access credentials are kept on that server only.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not shared with third parties beyond the processing described above, and not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Charles Inbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
